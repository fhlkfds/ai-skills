---
name: grill-me
description: Relentless interview to stress-test a plan or idea. Only run when the user asks.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Interview me about this plan until we share one understanding of it.

- Ask in rounds. Each round holds every question you can ask now without
  needing an answer from the same round. Number each question and give your
  recommended answer.
- Look up facts yourself; only ask me for decisions. Never answer a decision
  for me.
- Point out when my answers contradict each other.
- When nothing is left to ask, list every decision we made, then wait for me
  to confirm. Don't act until I do.
